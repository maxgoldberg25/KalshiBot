What are Estimated Signals?
Rows marked ~EST mean Kalshi has no active market makers for that contract — the orderbook is empty. To still show you useful data, the system takes real DraftKings odds from ESPN (free feed), converts them to a fair probability (removing the sportsbook's built-in juice), then simulates what a Kalshi price might look like if a market opened at ~90% of fair value — a common discount to attract initial buyers.

Think of these as watchlist items: they answer "if Kalshi opened this market at a discount today, would there be edge?" When Kalshi actually posts live prices, the system will automatically compare those real prices against these same sportsbook odds and surface genuine opportunities.

⚠ Do not execute estimated signals — there is no live Kalshi market to trade against.
